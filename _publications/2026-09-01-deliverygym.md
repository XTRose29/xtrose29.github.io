DeliveryGym is an RL environment for long-horizon embodied agent planning with an adaptive curriculum.

My contributions to DeliveryGym / DeliveryBench include visual traffic and safety infrastructure, an Unreal Engine first-person-view observation pipeline, and CityCore Paris map integration.
