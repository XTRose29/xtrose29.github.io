03 / Important assumptions

The last two pages of the supplied PDF (p.6, orchestra/loge; p.7, balcony) are the displayed seat-layout references. The first/second-floor exit diagrams (p.4–5) inform approximate routes, but are not spatially registered to the seat drawings. Seat coordinates are approximate, and exit markers are conceptual waypoints, not certified door coordinates. Each occupant moves toward an approximate aisle/exit, with limited exit throughput. No calibrated density, collisions, exact door widths, accessibility-specific paths, or realistic stair physics. Compare scenarios qualitatively only, not as real evacuation clearance predictions.
